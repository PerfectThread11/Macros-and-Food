SituationWhat happens

Keeping Your Recurring Spot

Consistency helps us provide you with a more reliable cleaner, arrival time, and schedule, which is ideal for you and us, honestly.

If you skip or reschedule more than 2 cleanings within 90 days, we will reach out to make sure your current schedule is still the right fit. If frequent changes continue, we may take you off your recurring spot and schedule future cleanings based on availability.

We understand that life happens, and we will always consider individual circumstances.

Pausing Your Service

Need to take a break? You can pause recurring service for up to 90 days.

To keep your recurring spot, your next cleaning must be scheduled within 90 days of your last cleaning. After 90 days, we will release the spot and can get you back on the schedule based on availability when you are ready to return. It's not that we don't want to keep you on the schedule; we just want to keep the schedule running as smoothly as possible.

Things That Affect Your Pricing

First-Time Cleaning

First-time cleans are priced higher (typically $50 to $150 more). Your routine cleaning price is based on the time needed for routine maintenance cleaning. The first cleaning requires a little extra love and can take longer.

Clutter and Extra Work

Excessive clutter or an unreasonable number of items to pick up or put away may require extra time and will be billed at $60/hour. This may affect what can be completed during the visit.

Additional Fees

Some deep-clean tasks have additional fees. These fees are outlined in our cleaning list. Please contact us if you would like a copy.

If your home requires more time than estimated, we will reach out to discuss whether to cap the cleaning at the scheduled time or extend it at our hourly rate.

Routine Customers

Skipped cleanings may result in a higher rate at the next visit. For example, weekly or bi-weekly clients going 3 or more weeks between cleanings will be charged the monthly rate quoted for their home.

Rate Adjustments

  • Rates are typically reviewed and adjusted once per year.
  • We may also review pricing if your home consistently takes significantly more or less time than originally quoted.
  • In rare cases, rate increases may happen outside the annual review due to extenuating circumstances (for example, rising costs, supply issues, or global events).

If you have questions about a rate adjustment, please reach out directly. We strive to keep pricing fair, transparent, and reflective of our service quality.

Lockouts and Access Issues

A lockout fee of 100% of the scheduled service price may apply if we cannot complete the cleaning due to access issues, including but not limited to:

  • Locked doors or incorrect entry codes
  • Alarm issues
  • Snow or ice that has not been cleared, making access unsafe
  • Active smoking inside the home, which requires us to leave

If we can return and clean later the same day, no fee applies, though cleaning time may be reduced.

If we are unable to access the home that day, or if you cancel less than 2 hours before your arrival window, the lockout fee applies.

Staff Safety

Our team members must feel safe at all times while working in your home. If a cleaner feels unsafe due to aggressive or uncontrolled animals, inappropriate or uncomfortable behavior, harassment, or improperly secured weapons, we reserve the right to leave immediately.

In these situations, the full service price will be charged. This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Aggressive or unsecured animals
  • Inappropriate comments, advances, or behavior
  • Firearms or other weapons not safely stored

Damages

We cover accidental damages caused by our staff when items were properly installed, in good condition, and not already loose, fragile, or poorly mounted.

Items not typically covered include, but are not limited to:

  • Blinds
  • Items not securely attached
  • Pre-damaged or unstable décor

For high-value items, we may request a visit to assess the damage before determining next steps.

Any damage not reported at the time of service must be reported within 48 hours so we can investigate and determine an appropriate resolution, such as repair or replacement.

Services We Do Not Offer

  • Tasks requiring ladders: anything beyond a 3-step stool for deep cleaning, or beyond a 1-step stool for general cleaning
  • Wall washing or full wall scrub-downs
  • Electronic screens (TVs, computers, monitors)
  • Laundry (washing, folding, putting away)
  • Starting appliances (washer, dryer, dishwasher, etc.)
  • Exterior cleaning of any kind (windows, siding, garages, patios)
  • Window track cleaning
  • Litter boxes or pet messes (urine, feces, vomit)
  • Biohazards (mold, blood, bodily fluids, hoarding-level waste)
  • Inside of china or curio cabinets
  • Washing aluminum or plastic blinds
  • Moving large furniture (anything heavier than a bar stool or dining chair)
  • Moving appliances to clean behind them

Certain tasks (for example, hazardous materials, mold removal, biohazards, infestations, or exterior windows) may require a specialist. We will let you know if something is outside our scope.
